## Before asking

Three steps save everyone time — and usually solve the problem:

1. **Reproduce it minimally.** Cut the code or command down to the smallest thing that still fails.
2. **Read the error message completely**, including the file and line number. Most messages say
   exactly what is wrong.
3. **Check [VERSIONS.md](VERSIONS.md).** A large share of "the tutorial is broken" reports are a
   tool version mismatch between the lesson and your machine.

Then include in your issue: your OS, the exact command you ran, the full error text, the lesson
path, and what you expected to happen.

## What is supported

- This repository's own material: lessons, exercises, project briefs, scripts in `scripts/`.
- Corrections and clarification of anything in the repository.

## What is **not** supported

- **Personal tutoring, code review of your private projects, or homework/assignment solving.**
- Career advice tailored to your individual situation (general guidance lives in
  [19-PROFESSIONAL-DEVELOPMENT](19-PROFESSIONAL-DEVELOPMENT/README.md)).
- Guaranteed response times. This is a single-maintainer project maintained in spare time.
- Issues about third-party services, frameworks, or cloud providers — their trackers are the right
  place, and they are better staffed.

## Honest expectations

The repository is written to be complete enough to study on its own, and honest about what is
unfinished: [ISSUE-REGISTER.md](ISSUE-REGISTER.md) lists every lesson and its status, and the
completion report discloses gaps rather than hiding them. If a lesson you need is marked as
planned, it is planned — not "coming soon".
